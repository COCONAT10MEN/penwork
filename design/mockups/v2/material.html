Glass Expressive

Material 3 Expressive structure · tinted liquid-glass surfaces · solid accents

Three glass strengths

Thin Chips and status over slides. Short labels only.
Blur 16 · fill 50%
Regular The dock, top bar, side panels and cards.
Blur 24 · fill 70%
Thick Popovers, menus, the inspector: anything with lots of text.
Blur 32 · fill 88%

Anatomy of one glass surface

4 3 1 2
  1. Tinted fill. Surface-container colour with a little primary mixed in.
  2. Backdrop blur + saturate. The colours behind glow through.
  3. Rim light. 1px edge plus a bright top line.
  4. Specular sheen. Soft white on the top half.
  5. Soft shadow. Only on floating things.

The frame is glass, the jewel is solid

Containers are glass. Anything selected or primary is a solid Material colour, and it morphs from a rounded square to a circle. That keeps it readable on any slide.

Motion

  • The logo and toolbar are one body. The capsule grows out of the logo with no seam and no bounce.
  • Selection slides between tools as one shape (shared-layout spring).
  • Popovers grow from the button they belong to and never cover the dock.
  • Small buttons may overshoot slightly (expressive). The dock and panels never do.

Fallbacks

Reduce transparency
Every glass surface becomes a solid tonal surface with the same shape.
Slow GPU
At most ~6 blurred layers at once. Popovers can switch to solid if WebView2 misdraws them.
