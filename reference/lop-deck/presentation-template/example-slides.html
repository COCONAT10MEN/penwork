SESSION1Newton’s Second Law
Part 01

Force and acceleration

▶Definition

The net force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration.

▶What it means

  • Bigger force → bigger acceleration
  • Bigger mass → smaller acceleration
  • Acceleration points along the net force

Part 02

The formula

▶Start from the law

\Sigma F = m a
Solve for the acceleration:
a = \frac{F}{m}
Units: 1\ \u{N} = 1\ \u{kg\cdot m/s^2}

Solved Ex A net force of 12 N acts on a 3 kg trolley. Find its acceleration.

Answer

a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{12}{3} = 4\ \u{m/s^2}
